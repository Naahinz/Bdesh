Privacy notice

Updated 3 November 2026 · Bdesh public beta

Bdesh is a student-led, independent admissions preparation website. You can read the learning guide without signing in. If you choose to sign in, we use your information to save and display your personal admissions workspace.

Information we process

  • Account details: Supabase Authentication processes sign-in credentials, email and, when using Google, account information such as your name and profile image shared by the provider.
  • Academic and planning information you enter: academic stage, school, graduation year, scores, intended subjects, country preferences, activities, honors, essays and drafts, personal statements or academic context, preparedness milestones, university applications, document statuses, deadlines and notes.
  • Local browser data: reading progress and authentication session information may be kept in browser storage to support continuity.
  • Basic technical information: our hosting, authentication and content-delivery providers may process network and service logs needed to deliver and secure the site.

How we use information

To authenticate you, save your work, restore it across sessions, display progress, support document tracking and allow you to export your data. Bdesh does not automatically submit applications or financial-aid forms to universities. We do not sell student data to advertisers.

Access and service providers

Personal student records are protected by account-ownership permissions in Supabase. The college information directory is shared research content; authorized Bdesh editors can update those shared entries but are not granted access to student application records through the editorial interface. We use Supabase (project hosted in the EU/Ireland region) for account and database services, Cloudflare Pages for website hosting, Google for optional sign-in, and a content-delivery network for the Supabase browser library. These providers have their own practices and policies.

Optional product analytics

Bdesh may offer an opt-in PostHog analytics feature to help us understand which public pages are read and which application-preparation steps are completed. Analytics is disabled until the visitor explicitly chooses to allow it. When activated, it records only predefined event names (such as a lesson number or a successfully added application), not the content of personal essays, academic scores, searched university names, passwords or other form entries. We disable click/form autocapture, session replay and automatic pageview capture, and do not link PostHog events to student names, email addresses or Supabase account IDs. PostHog may process network information needed to deliver events according to its own policies. Your selection is stored in your browser as `bdesh_analytics_consent_v1`; you can clear this site storage to choose again. The PostHog project is not activated until its project token and region are configured.

Feedback form and email delivery

When you submit the feedback form, your message, selected topic, optional reply email address and form page address are sent to FormSubmit, a third-party form-to-email provider, which routes the message to eftekhernahin@gmail.com after the recipient activates the delivery address. FormSubmit may run spam checks and retains form submissions for up to 30 days according to its documentation. You can choose not to use this form and contact the team by email instead. Do not include passwords or sensitive student records in feedback.

What not to upload or include

Bdesh is not a secure tax-document submission portal. Do not paste passwords, passports, bank account numbers, tax returns, identity document images or other highly sensitive records into free-text fields. Upload financial-aid documents directly to the college's authorized portal instead.

Your choices

You may edit saved information in your account, download workspace data, and save a profile PDF locally through your browser. You can sign out at any time. To request deletion of your Bdesh account and associated records, email nahineftekher@gmail.com from your account email. Deletion requests are handled manually in this beta, and this is not yet a self-service feature. Some security logs and provider backups may follow their own retention schedules.

Younger students

Students should only create an account when legally eligible to do so and with any parental or guardian permission required where they live. Students who cannot register may still read the free guide without an account.

Contact and changes

For questions, privacy requests or corrections, contact nahineftekher@gmail.com. This notice may be revised as Bdesh's features or services change; the updated date will be shown here.
