Bdesh beta privacy notice

Last updated: November 1, 2026. Bdesh is a student-led, free undergraduate guidance prototype. Account creation is available to students who can lawfully register and have any required parental or guardian permission. Students who do not have this permission can browse and use the local-only roadmap without registering.

What we store

When you register, Supabase Auth stores your email and authentication details. Bdesh stores your selected academic stage, group, intended university intake, preferred language, and completed roadmap tasks. Unregistered visitors' checklist progress remains in their own browser.

Why

To authenticate accounts and restore your personal roadmap across devices. We do not request identity documents, home addresses or family financial records. We do not sell your data.

Service providers

Account data is stored in a Supabase project located in the EU (Ireland). Website hosting is provided separately. The website loads a Supabase JavaScript library from a third-party content delivery network.

Control and deletion

You can sign out at any time. For account or data deletion requests, email nahineftekher@gmail.com. Deletion is handled manually in this beta, not yet through a self-service control. This limitation must be addressed before a wider launch.

Important

Information here is educational and may change. Verify university application eligibility, costs and deadlines on official websites.
