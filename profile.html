03 / ACTIVITIES

Your life beyond class.

The U.S. Common App first-year activities section allows up to 10 entries. Bdesh helps you prepare each entry with concise, copy-ready fields—plus a separate space for the full story.

Common App-style fields

Activity type, role (up to 50 characters), organization (up to 100), brief description (up to 150), grade levels, participation timing, hours per week and weeks per year. Follow the live Common App for its final questions.

My activity list 0 / 10

Beyond the short description

Use the detailed notes in every activity to explain what you did, what changed, evidence of impact, lessons learned, and what you want to do next. Those notes are yours; they're not restricted to 150 characters.

05 / WRITING

Find your voice.

Keep personal statements and application essay drafts here. In the real Common App, essay requirements vary by college. Use this as a private draft notebook—not as an automatic submission tool.

Personal essay guidance

The Common App first-year personal essay has a 650-word maximum where used; some colleges make it optional. Bdesh lets you save longer early drafts so you can revise before copying your final text.
